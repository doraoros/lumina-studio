    How it decides

    A pipeline, not a simulated score.

    The 3,000-frame demo starts from real wedding photographs. Each photo becomes a few compositions, near-identical bursts, and failed frames — motion blur or bad exposure — so there is a real volume to cull. Labels exist only to measure how often the pipeline is right.

    1. Sharpness. Laplacian variance. Blurred frames drop out.
    2. Exposure. Mean luminance and clipped highlights or shadows.
    3. Duplicates. 64-bit dHash. A small Hamming distance means a burst.
    4. Faces. YuNet detector, SFace embeddings, cosine grouping.
    5. Album. The best frames, at most two per scene, across four sections.
    6. Economics. Manual hours minus measured runtime and the photographer’s review.
